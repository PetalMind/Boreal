Boreal interface sounds

The bundled WAV files are adapted from CC0 sound effects published on
OpenGameArt.org. Attribution is not required, but the original sources are
kept here for provenance.

- boreal-confirmation.wav: Beep Tone Sound SFX by qubodup
  https://opengameart.org/content/beep-tone-sound-sfx
- boreal-success.wav: Bell Arpeggio 24 by cynicmusic
  https://opengameart.org/content/bell-arpeggio-24
- boreal-launch.wav, boreal-task-start.wav, boreal-task-complete.wav,
  boreal-warning.wav, boreal-error.wav: Atmospheric Interaction Sound Pack by
  legoluft, submitted by qubodup
  https://opengameart.org/content/atmospheric-interaction-sound-pack

All listed sources are marked CC0 on their OpenGameArt pages.
